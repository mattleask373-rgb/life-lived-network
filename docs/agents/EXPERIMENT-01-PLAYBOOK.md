# Experiment 01 — Cross-Industry Income Stream Playbook

**State: DESIGN / READY FOR HUMAN-GATED EXPERIMENTATION / NOT LIVE**

The first experiment cycle is deliberately small, reversible and evidence-first.
No paid acquisition is required.

## 1. Local opportunity leads

**Customer:** small local operators  
**Hypothesis:** verified local opportunity packets can create qualified demand signals.  
**Smallest test:** produce one evidence-backed opportunity packet from canonical discovery and have a human reviewer assess relevance.  
**Success:** reviewer confirms relevance and a real action follows.  
**Failure:** weak evidence, irrelevant match, stale supply, or no useful action.  
**Payment hypothesis:** lead/referral.  
**Gate:** human approval before any external contact or commercial handoff.

## 2. Provider intelligence

**Customer:** small expert teams  
**Hypothesis:** recurring verified intelligence saves enough decision time to justify a subscription.  
**Smallest test:** produce one dated, source-linked intelligence packet and obtain human customer-value feedback.  
**Success:** confirmed usefulness plus a repeatable information need.  
**Failure:** low novelty, weak verification, excessive delivery cost, or no repeat need.  
**Payment hypothesis:** subscription.  
**Gate:** human reviews factual claims before delivery.

## 3. Opportunity research kits

**Customer:** small businesses evaluating markets  
**Hypothesis:** evidence packets reduce research effort enough to justify a service fee.  
**Smallest test:** one bounded market question, one evidence packet, one human review.  
**Success:** decision usefulness is confirmed and the workflow is repeatable.  
**Failure:** unsupported conclusions, ambiguous evidence, or excessive manual cost.  
**Payment hypothesis:** service.  
**Gate:** human approves every external deliverable.

## 4. Partner discovery

**Customer:** community/network operators  
**Hypothesis:** verified capability maps reveal partner candidates that are difficult to discover manually.  
**Smallest test:** map one partner category using public evidence only.  
**Success:** a human confirms fit and requests a next step.  
**Failure:** speculative fit, stale evidence, or no actionable candidate.  
**Payment hypothesis:** licensing.  
**Gate:** human approves all partner contact and commercial terms.

## 5. Event/locality intelligence

**Customer:** event and hospitality operators  
**Hypothesis:** verified locality/event context can improve commercial decisions without fabricating inventory.  
**Smallest test:** produce one locality intelligence packet from canonical locality/world evidence.  
**Success:** a real operator confirms the packet changes or improves a decision.  
**Failure:** sparse/stale evidence, no actionable insight, or unsupported claims.  
**Payment hypothesis:** referral.  
**Gate:** human reviews referral terms and any external publication.

## Common evidence ledger

Every experiment records:

- hypothesis;
- source evidence and timestamps;
- epistemic state;
- uncertainty;
- customer/problem evidence;
- action taken;
- observed outcome;
- independent verification where material;
- cost to deliver;
- reversibility;
- kill decision;
- next question.

## Portfolio rules

- Zero paid spend in the first cycle.
- No autonomous outreach.
- No autonomous contracts.
- No fabricated customer, demand, revenue, conversion or retention claims.
- UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.
- A successful experiment does not automatically become LIVE.
- A failed experiment is useful evidence and should update the frontier.

## Promotion gate

A stream may move from EXPERIMENTAL only after evidence supports:

1. real customer value;
2. repeatable demand/acquisition;
3. truthful payment mechanism;
4. acceptable delivery economics;
5. safe authority boundaries;
6. independent review of material claims.

Until then, the stream remains an experiment.
