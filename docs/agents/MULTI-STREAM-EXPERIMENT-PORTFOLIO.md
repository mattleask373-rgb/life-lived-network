# Multi-Stream Experiment Portfolio

**State: DESIGN / NOT LIVE**

This is the economic experimentation boundary for the fleet. It defines repeatable,
bounded stream kits without claiming customers, revenue, rankings, traffic, or live
market demand.

## Operating rule

One fleet substrate can support adjacent experiments across industries, but each
stream must have its own:

- customer and problem definition;
- value proposition;
- payment mechanism;
- evidence requirements;
- kill criteria;
- autonomy ceiling;
- human approval gates.

The system may discover and prepare opportunities. It may not silently publish,
contact prospects, spend money, create contracts, or represent unverified demand as
fact.

## Initial portfolio hypotheses

| Stream | Industry | Payment | Smallest safe test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local opportunity leads | Local services | Lead | Draft one evidence-backed opportunity packet |
| Provider intelligence | Professional services | Subscription | Produce one verified weekly market-intelligence packet |
| Opportunity research kits | Small businesses | Service | Deliver one human-reviewed research brief |
| Partner/white-label discovery | Communities | Licensing | Draft one partner-specific capability map |
| Event/locality intelligence | Events & hospitality | Referral | Validate one verified locality/event demand path |

These are **hypotheses**, not validated businesses.

## Portfolio kill rule

A stream stays experimental until evidence demonstrates all of:

1. a real customer problem;
2. a credible value signal;
3. a repeatable acquisition path;
4. a truthful payment mechanism;
5. acceptable delivery cost;
6. no unresolved safety or authority boundary.

Kill or redesign a stream when its evidence fails the hypothesis. Do not keep weak
streams alive because they sound commercially attractive.

## Agent-team division

- **ChatGPT:** product contract, experiment design, SEO/growth drafts, adversarial economics.
- **Grok:** control-plane fencing, recovery, identity, execution safety.
- **Lovable:** surface/UI experiments against approved APIs.
- **Human:** approvals, credentials, paid activity, external publication, contracts and production activation.

## First experiment phase

Experiments should begin with zero paid spend and human-reviewed drafts. The first
objective is evidence of customer value, not revenue theatre.

No stream becomes LIVE merely because a code path exists.
