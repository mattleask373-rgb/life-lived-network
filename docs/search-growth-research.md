# Search Growth Research Operating Model

## Purpose
Living World should use search demand as a research signal for product and locality expansion, not as a page-generation target.

## Evidence baseline
Current research reviewed October 2026 indicates:
- Google remains the dominant starting point for local business search, while consumers commonly move across multiple channels.
- Local search is strongly mobile-led.
- Proximity, complete information, reviews, and clear business information materially affect local decision-making.
- “Near me” remains useful intent evidence, but exact phrase stuffing is not the strategy; modern search systems infer local intent without requiring the literal phrase.
- Google explicitly warns against doorway pages and scaled content abuse. AI-assisted or automated production is not itself the problem; producing large amounts of low-value content primarily to manipulate rankings is.

## Strategic thesis
Prioritise local intent + real evidence + actionability over raw keyword volume.

High-value intent families:
1. What's on / what's happening
2. Things to do / local guides
3. Local services and professionals
4. Jobs and work opportunities
5. Classes, workshops and experiences
6. Community projects and contribution
7. Journey / route discovery
8. Time-specific discovery: tonight, tomorrow, weekend, after work
9. Help / people who can help

## Search intelligence loop
SEARCH SIGNAL → INTENT CLUSTER → LOCALITY → REAL-WORLD EVIDENCE → CANONICAL DISCOVERY → USEFUL PAGE/ENTRY → ACTION → NETWORK PARTICIPATION

## Research rules
- Search demand signals require source and observation date.
- Do not fabricate search volume.
- Historical signals are evidence, not guarantees of future demand.
- First-party Living World query data should eventually be treated as a high-value signal.
- Demand without real supply should create a supply-acquisition opportunity, not a fake page.
- Real supply without sufficient demand may still be strategically valuable, but should not be justified by invented search volume.
- Search variants should be clustered into intents; do not create one page per wording variant.

## Locality strategy
Treat locality × intent as the expansion unit. A locality should be activated when it has enough real data to answer a meaningful user question.

Candidate surfaces include:
- /events/{locality}
- /things-to-do/{locality}
- /jobs/{locality}
- /gardeners/{locality}
- /sports-massage/{locality}
- /community-projects/{locality}

These are examples only. Indexability must be gated by evidence, freshness, usefulness and actionability.

## Growth worker roles
Future bounded workers may research:
- search demand
- local intent clusters
- supply gaps
- locality readiness
- content/guide opportunities
- business/provider acquisition opportunities
- freshness and stale pages
- SEO quality
- conversion/actionability
- network-effect opportunities

Workers create evidence-backed opportunities/tasks. They do not autonomously invent data or bypass human/security/CI gates.

## Quality gate
A local surface should be indexable only when it has:
- canonical route
- meaningful locality
- real evidence
- useful main content
- provenance where relevant
- freshness appropriate to the subject
- at least one legitimate user action or useful next step
- no misleading availability/qualification/endorsement claims

Otherwise prefer NOINDEX, DRAFT, or no page.

## Product principle
The objective is not traffic for traffic's sake. The objective is REAL_WORLD_ACTION: attend, work, help, connect, learn, contribute, visit, plan or go.
