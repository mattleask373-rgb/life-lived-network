# EXPERIMENT-01 — Non-production experiment packets

Status: DESIGN / PREP ONLY

This packet operationalizes issue #114 without activating any stream. It is compatible with PR #113's bounded, human-gated experiment contracts.

## Global gates

- Zero paid spend.
- No credentials or secrets created.
- No external outreach, publication, contracts, or production execution.
- No autonomous merge/deploy.
- Human approval is required before any external action.
- No claim of customer demand, revenue, traffic, rankings, conversion, retention, or validation without independent evidence.
- Any unresolved authority, scope, security, or hosted-verification condition is a HOLD.
- Each experiment is reversible and may be stopped without downstream production impact.

## Evidence record

For every test, record: experiment id, hypothesis, target segment, proposed smallest test, start/end timestamps, exact inputs, outputs, evidence links, human approver, costs (must be $0), anomalies, decision, and next action.

## Packet 01 — Local opportunity leads

Hypothesis: A narrow local-service operator has a recurring need for a small, specific opportunity list that saves research time.

Smallest non-production test: prepare one synthetic/example opportunity brief from already-available repository-safe/public data structures, clearly marked as a test artifact. Do not contact or represent any real prospect.

Success signals: brief is understandable, scoped, reproducible, and an evaluator can identify a plausible time-saving outcome.

Failure/kill: unclear buyer/problem, unverifiable source data, scope ambiguity, or any need for unapproved external contact.

Decision gate: human review of artifact quality only; no outreach.

## Packet 02 — Provider intelligence

Hypothesis: A professional-services provider can benefit from a compact, evidence-linked intelligence brief about a defined opportunity class.

Smallest non-production test: construct one offline sample brief using a fixed schema and synthetic fixtures; measure completeness, source traceability, and preparation effort.

Success signals: traceable evidence, repeatable structure, bounded preparation cost, no unsupported claims.

Failure/kill: missing provenance, unverifiable assertions, or schema requiring hidden authority.

Decision gate: human review before any real-provider use.

## Packet 03 — Opportunity research kits

Hypothesis: A small business can receive value from a reusable research kit for one narrowly defined decision.

Smallest non-production test: create one decision kit containing question set, evidence checklist, research template, and example output using synthetic inputs.

Success signals: a reviewer can complete the decision workflow without invented facts or hidden dependencies.

Failure/kill: ambiguous decision owner, unsupported data, excessive manual cost, or need for production integration.

Decision gate: human approval before applying to any real business.

## Packet 04 — Partner discovery

Hypothesis: A community/operator can benefit from a structured shortlist of potentially relevant partner categories.

Smallest non-production test: generate a taxonomy and synthetic shortlist examples; validate the matching criteria and evidence ledger without contacting any entity.

Success signals: criteria are explicit, false-positive risks are visible, and every proposed field has a provenance rule.

Failure/kill: implied endorsement, fabricated partner fit, or any requirement for unapproved outreach.

Decision gate: human review before any real-world discovery or contact.

## Packet 05 — Event/locality intelligence

Hypothesis: An event or hospitality operator can benefit from a narrow locality intelligence brief for a defined planning decision.

Smallest non-production test: produce one offline brief from synthetic locality/event records, with every field labelled as fixture data.

Success signals: clear decision usefulness, deterministic structure, and explicit UNKNOWN states where evidence is absent.

Failure/kill: stale/unverifiable inputs, ambiguous locality authority, or need to publish/index externally.

Decision gate: human review; no publication or production execution.
