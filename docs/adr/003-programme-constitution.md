# ADR 003: Human–AI Symbiotic Intelligence & Discovery Programme Constitution

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-10-07

## Decision

The Human–AI Symbiotic Intelligence & Discovery Programme is governed by the canonical constitution in:

`docs/programme/MASTER-PROGRAMME-SPECIFICATION.md`

The programme is a living intelligence-development operating system rather than a fixed course.

Its core loop is:

**PURPOSE → POSSIBILITY → CAPABILITY → INTELLIGENCE → ACTION → REALITY → EVIDENCE → LEARNING → EXPANSION**

The programme must remain implementation-fluid while preserving constitutional principles around human agency, epistemic honesty, safety, privacy, security, reversibility and human authority over consequential decisions.

## Architectural principles

- Human purpose is constitutional authority.
- AI expands possibility but does not silently rewrite the constitution.
- Projects are anchors, not curriculum boundaries.
- Capability discovery is a first-class capability.
- Evidence and uncertainty are explicit.
- Unknown is a valid state.
- Experiments are preferred to unsupported certainty.
- Existing canonical mechanisms must be extended before new authorities are created.
- Expansion proposals become ordinary bounded tasks after acceptance.
- Autonomous production merge/deploy authority remains disabled unless separately authorised.
- Programme autonomy and product autonomy are distinct concerns.

## Consequences

The programme can evolve beyond its original educational categories without becoming uncontrolled self-modifying software.

Future AI agents can use the constitution as their mission and operating context while relying on the existing durable fleet control plane for bounded execution, claims, leases, handoffs, verification and human gates.

## First executable seams

1. AI-NATIVE-01 — bounded expansion proposal kernel.
2. AI-NATIVE-02 — programme state seam.
3. Later seams are generated only after evidence from preceding work.

## Non-goals

This ADR does not authorise:

- a new graph database;
- vector recommendation infrastructure;
- a second discovery/matcher authority;
- speculative learner profiling;
- autonomous production changes;
- weakening security/RLS/privacy;
- fabrication of real-world facts;
- uncontrolled self-modification.
