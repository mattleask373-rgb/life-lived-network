# Revenue experiment plan (hypotheses — none launched, no revenue claimed)

Ethics gate for every stream: no selling or targeting with private place memories, no paid ranking
inside findSupply(), no guaranteed outcomes, labelled sponsorship only, human approval before any charge.

| # | Stream | Hypothesis | Cheapest test | Pass signal (internal guess) |
|---|--------|-----------|---------------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Founding Local Partner (€19/mo) | Local orgs pay for listing help + feedback voice | 15–20 outreach conversations | 3 price commitments subject to written terms |
| 2 | Listing setup service (one-off) | Orgs pay once for an accurate, checked listing | Ask in interview Q4 | 3 of 8 say yes at a stated price |
| 3 | Community/council pilot | Councils/charities fund local coverage | 3 warm intros, one-page proposal | 1 meeting with budget holder |
| 4 | Event organiser tools | Organisers pay for verified event publishing | 5 organiser interviews | 2 willing to trial |
| 5 | Supporter membership | Users pay to sustain an ad-free, private atlas | Copy test on a "support" note (no payment) | Qualitative only until payments approved |

Sequence: 1 → 2 (same interviews) → 3 → 4 → 5. Payments require a separate approved plan.
Sponsorship must never alter discovery order or be shown without a "Sponsored" label.
