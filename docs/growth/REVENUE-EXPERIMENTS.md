# 30-day revenue validation plan (hypotheses — nothing launched, no revenue claimed)

Ethics gate (every day): never use private place memories for targeting; no paid ranking inside
findSupply(); no guaranteed outcomes; no charges until a separate payments plan is approved.
Record outcomes in /partner-tracker (this device only). Targets are internal guesses, not benchmarks.

## Pricing hypotheses
| Stream | Hypothesis (cents, unvalidated) | What would falsify it |
|---|---|---|
| Founding Local Partner | 1900/month | <3 of 8 interviewees say yes subject to terms |
| Listing setup service | one-off 4900 | Nobody names it as worth paying for |
| Community/council pilot | 50000–200000 per locality/quarter | No budget holder will meet |
| Event organiser tools | 900/month | Organisers say existing free tools suffice |

## Weekly goals
- **Week 1 — list & reach:** 20 named organisations in tracker (real, public info only); 10 contacted.
- **Week 2 — talk:** 15–20 contacted; 5 interviews done; price asked in every interview.
- **Week 3 — test price & service:** 8–10 interviews; ask about setup service; 3 council/charity intros.
- **Week 4 — decide:** tally scorecard; write a one-page decision memo per stream.

## Daily actions (~60 minutes)
1. Check "Follow-ups due" in the tracker; clear every overdue item.
2. Send 2–3 first messages using `OUTREACH-KIT.md`.
3. Log every reply, interview and price answer the same day, verbatim where possible.
4. Set a next action and follow-up date on every active row.

## Decision gates
- **Day 10:** <5 replies from 10 contacts → rewrite the first message before sending more.
- **Day 20:** <3 interviews → change channel (in person, via local networks).
- **Day 30:** ≥3 price yeses (terms pending) → draft written trial terms and a payments plan for
  human approval. 1–2 → retest at another price or bundle. 0 → pause the stream; keep learning.
Never promote a stream to paid without written terms, legal review and explicit approval.

## Unit-economics questions to answer (not assumptions)
- How many hours does onboarding one partner listing actually take?
- What does keeping a listing accurate cost per month in team time?
- How many partners per locality before the map stays useful without paid help?
- Expected churn reason: what would make a partner cancel after month 1?
- Payment provider fees and VAT treatment at 1900 cents (to research before any payments plan).
- Can a council pilot cover the cost of making a quiet locality useful for everyone?
