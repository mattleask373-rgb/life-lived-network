# Founding Local Partner — outreach kit (validation only)

Rules: no promises of traffic, leads or sales. €19/month is a hypothesis. Nothing is for sale.
Never mention or use anyone's private place memories. Replace [brackets] by hand; never invent facts.

## First message (email or in person)
Hi [name], I'm [your name], working on Real World Atlas — a map of what's genuinely happening in
[locality]. We haven't launched anything paid. We're asking a few local organisations for a
20-minute chat about how people find you today and whether anything we're building would help.
No commitment, nothing to buy. Would [day] suit? Details: https://life-lived-network.lovable.app/partners

## Follow-up (once, after 5–7 days)
Hi [name], just a gentle nudge on the 20-minute chat. Completely fine if it's not a good time.

## Interview guide (20 minutes)
1. How do people find you today? What works, what doesn't?
2. What have you paid for before to be found (ads, directories)? Was it worth it?
3. Show the draft listing. What's wrong or missing?
4. "If this cost €19/month, what would it need to do for that to be obviously worth it?"
5. Would you agree to €19/month subject to written terms? (Record yes/no/maybe — not a payment.)
