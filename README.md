THE LIVING WORLD

A real-life network for people, places, possibility and human connection

READ THIS BEFORE BUILDING ANYTHING

We are creating something fundamentally different from a conventional social network, travel app, marketplace or AI assistant.

This is a platform designed around a simple belief:

Technology should help people live more of their lives in the real world.

The product should help people:

discover places

meet people

find work

discover experiences

find music

discover artists

join communities

support projects

exchange skills

contribute time

travel with purpose

create things together

find belonging

The ultimate goal is not to increase screen time.

The ultimate goal is to increase:

life lived.

37. THE PHILOSOPHICAL INSPIRATION

The platform may draw philosophical inspiration from ideas explored in works such as The Celestine Prophecy, especially the notion of an emerging culture in which technology frees humans to focus more on meaningful encounters, contribution, nature and personal purpose.

Do not present these philosophical ideas as scientific facts.

Translate the useful principle into product design:

Technology should handle more of the coordination so humans have more space for living, creating, connecting and contributing.

The product should make:

finding opportunities easier

finding people easier

planning journeys easier

translating easier

coordinating easier

discovering communities easier

finding meaningful contribution easier

so that the human can spend less time organising life and more time actually living it.
