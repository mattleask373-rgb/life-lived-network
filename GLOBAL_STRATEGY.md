# Global Strategy — One Verified Locality at a Time

**Status:** strategic operating contract; it is not launch, spending, outreach, legal or deployment authorization.

## North star

Make it easier for people in villages, towns, neighbourhoods and cities—both long-term locals and travelers—to find a genuine, current, eligible way to help, learn, meet or participate, and then return to real life. The app should be useful and calm, not sticky.

## Principles

- Free core mutual aid forever; no pay-to-play for basic village visibility.
- One central live map and one canonical place/supply system.
- Local and traveler value must be reciprocal. Local stewardship can constrain what is shown.
- Build in dependency order: identity and privacy → data integrity → usability → verified pilot → careful locality expansion → bounded automation.
- No fabricated listings, invented local demand, unsupported supply counts, fake social proof or generic city-guide filler.
- AI is a bounded helper over eligible records, not a source of local facts and not an autonomous publisher.
- No claim of global coverage, massive capacity, 24/7 execution, or retention success without evidence.

## Expansion method

1. Verify current host/build/backend and access rules.
2. Establish one complete, safe supply → discovery → consented response → agreed plan → confirmed outcome loop.
3. Measure real human outcomes, safety, freshness, fairness, accessibility and cost.
4. Select the next locality using evidence, not founder intuition or search impressions alone.
5. Localise language, moderation, trust and local-owner controls with human review.
6. Expand only when the previous locality shows durable useful activity and has a real support/safety path.

## Research hypotheses — not selected markets

At most the following three hypotheses may be compared by the Monday research-only scout. This report does not declare a winner or claim demand/supply evidence:

1. Australian short-stay turnover cleaning.
2. One locality for gardening/light home-help.
3. Multilingual urban Spanish-learning exchange / low-risk local guide.

Use the scorecard weights: reachable real supply 25%; demonstrable local demand 20%; anchor participants 15%; safety/regulatory simplicity 15%; repeat use 10%; lawful source/data path 10%; localisation/operations 5%.

For every factual claim record source URL, publisher, publication date, retrieval time, geography, language, confidence and whether the evidence directly supports the claim. Separate facts, hypotheses, unknowns and questions requiring interviews. Do not invent scores to fill missing evidence. Do not contact people, create accounts, publish, seed data, pay providers or make commitments.

## Pilot evidence protocol (future target, not current result)

- First interview 5–10 consenting participants across the relevant supply/demand sides; ask about last real occurrence, current workaround, time-to-response, cancellation, trust and what would make the exchange unsafe or not worthwhile.
- State five unresolved questions for each hypothesis and who could answer them.
- Track a target of 20 independent real-world signals as a future research milestone, not as proof of demand unless the source and independence are documented.
- Require opt-in supply and a human escalation route before any public pilot.
- Report denominator, missingness, selection bias, and contrary evidence alongside positive signals.

## Current phase

Tier 0 security/identity gates remain open. Public place-page generation, mass seeding, external discovery integrations, paid acquisition, external partner contact, public AI and unattended production automation remain frozen. Design and evidence-gathering are preparatory work only.
